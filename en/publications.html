Publications

Highlights

MBD8 is required for LDL2-mediated transcriptional repression downstream of H3K9me2 in Arabidopsis
Mori et al., Nucleic Acids Research, 2025 [Paper] [Press Release]
▶In Arabidopsis, H3K9me2 removes H3K4me1 through H3K4me1 demethylation by the histone demethylase LDL2, but the detailed molecular mechanism of this pathway was unknown. In this study, we showed from genetic and biochemical perspectives that a protein called methyl-CpG-binding domain protein 8 (MBD8) is involved in this pathway.
H3K4me2 orchestrates H2A.Z and Polycomb repressive marks in Arabidopsis
Noyori et al., Nature Communications, 2025 [Paper] [Press Release]
▶H3K4 methylation has generally been regarded as a mark of transcriptional activation. However, we revealed that in plants, H3K4me2 promotes the localization of histone modifications and histone variants that are closely associated with transcriptional repression.
H3K9 methylation regulates heterochromatin silencing through incoherent feedforward loops
Yabe et al., Science Advances, 2024 [Paper] [Press Release]
▶We reported that H3K9 methylation, which has been considered to be involved in transcriptional repression, also acts in plants to promote transcription via H3K36me3, a mark of transcriptional activation.
Cotranscriptional demethylation induces global loss of H3K4me2 from active genes in Arabidopsis
Mori et al., EMBO Journal, 2023 [Paper] [Press Release]
▶How does a gene remember the information that it "has been transcribed"? We revealed that in plants, the histone demethylase LDL3 removes H3K4me2 in coordination with the transcription machinery. Through this mechanism, transcribed genes are thought to be recorded in the genome as a "low-H3K4me2 state."
Transcription-coupled and epigenome-encoded mechanisms direct H3K4 methylation
Oya et al., Nature Communications, 2022 [Paper] [Press Release]
▶H3K4 methylation exists in three states, H3K4me1/me2/me3, but how their localization patterns are regulated in plants was unknown. In this paper, we identified three histone methyltransferases (ATX1, ATX2, and ATXR7) responsible for H3K4me1.
Chromatin-based mechanisms to coordinate convergent overlapping transcription
Inagaki et al., Nature Plants, 2021 [Paper]
▶We revealed that FLD (an H3K4me1 demethylase) is involved in regulating the transcription of two genes whose gene regions overlap.
